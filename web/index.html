About Me

I'm a self-taught software engineer who builds practical software across the full stack — from Python tooling to React/TypeScript dashboards to Flutter apps. Most of what I know I picked up by shipping real projects, not by following a curriculum, which means I care more about whether something works and holds up than about doing things the "textbook" way.

Lately I've been deep in AI-assisted workflows — building tools like DirScribe that help AI models understand codebases faster, and products like Soros Alpha and Ideaspark that put AI directly in front of users. I like taking a messy idea and turning it into something structured, fast, and actually usable.
