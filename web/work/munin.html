Munin

One calm feed for about 110 Indian newsrooms.

Stack
React 19, TypeScript, TanStack Start, RSS
Visit Site →

The problem

Keeping up with Indian news means a dozen tabs, three apps and a group chat forwarding screenshots. Nothing reads well across outlets, and nothing remembers what you already saw.

What I built

Munin pulls live headlines from around 110 Indian newsrooms and lays them out three ways — a chronological feed, a reels-style stack for skimming, and topic browsing. You can follow the outlets you trust, save stories for later, and write your own take on anything you read.

How I built it

React 19 + TanStack Start with SSR. Feeds are fetched and normalised server-side so the browser never talks to a dozen RSS endpoints, and reading state is persisted locally so a session survives a refresh.

Key decisions

  • Normalised every feed on the server — doing it client-side would have meant a dozen CORS failures and a slow first paint.
  • Built three reading modes because skimming, browsing and deep reading are different jobs.

Result

Live, with a catalogue of around 110 newsrooms and three reading modes over one normalised feed.
